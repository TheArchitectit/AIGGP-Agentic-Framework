# Archived OpenSpec sprint routing

This is the S1 intake queue for historical work. A directory in the archive records a past decision. To reopen work, the lead selects a specific unresolved claim, a Luna collector traces the original task and successor evidence, and a separate auditor verifies the disposition. The accountable owner and lead can then assign confirmed work to an active S2-S4 plan. Preserve the period record. [DG-04 execution plan](../changes/dg-04-archive-task-reconciliation/sprint-plan.md) and [sprint roles](../SPRINTS.md#dispatch-by-agent-type).

Same-name published capabilities provide a place to check current requirements. DG-04 tracks the original 111 unchecked or partial rows separately.
