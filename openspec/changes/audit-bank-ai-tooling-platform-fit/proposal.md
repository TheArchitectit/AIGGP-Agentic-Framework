# Audit bank AI tooling platform fit

## Why

The November 25 technical architecture identifies DevGate as the software
delivery control and assigns runtime authority to bank services. The project
needs a coverage decision and a qualification plan before it relies on any
component in production.

## What Changes

- Trace the technical architecture to available, proposed, and bank-owned
  capabilities.
- Record how a reference delivery workflow, runtime check boundary, and
  monitoring recovery would be qualified.
- Publish the authorized assessment and qualification plan while keeping
  bank-owned implementation, tenant configuration, access details, and
  acceptance evidence under the bank's control.

## Impact

Architecture and evidence planning. Bank systems, tenant policies, licences,
secrets, and workload access require their own project and verification.
