# Sprint plan — DG-06 art evolution external proof

**Scope:** complete the evidence disposition for the human/external-consumer obligation without inventing a title or local enforcement site.
Tasks 1.1–1.2 and 2.1/2.3 are recorded; current evidence identifies no consuming title or responsible individual.

## Sequence

### S1 — intake/baseline

- Re-read the registry event, prevention mapping, art guide, and external-proof disposition in `evidence.md`.
- Task 2.2 is the remaining task: verify the record names owner, scope, and applicable requirement, and does not claim local material proves external behavior.

### S2 — implementation or evidence collection

- Until an AIGGP maintainer identifies the consuming title and art/game owner, keep the owner **NOT IDENTIFIED** and external proof NOT_RUN.
- After that owner decision, collect only supplied consumer-repository revision and shipped-target build/test evidence directly connected to the art failure class and `art-evolve-01`.
- Record owner, target, evidence reference, and limits; do not create a source marker for a human-only process.

### S3 — independent qualification

- A distinct audit session checks that the cited consumer belongs to the named title, the supplied target result is real and revision-bound, and the disposition distinguishes local registry checks from external behavior.
- Stop if no owner/artifact is supplied or the record overstates what it proves; retain NOT_RUN and the requirement uncovered.

### S4 — integration/closeout

- Lead reconciles the record and signs off task 2.2 only when its ownership/scope/reference checks pass. Otherwise preserve the missing-owner disposition for later review.
- Keep `art-evolve-01` uncovered unless a real enforcing site is independently established; local generic registry tests do not close it.

## Bounded roles

- Lead: scope, review, and sign-off. Luna: verify local record fields and collect owner-supplied references without contacting an unselected consumer.
- The actual consuming title’s art/game owner is the acceptance owner once identified; a separate audit session checks the supplied proof.
